## 🧩 About

**MU Online Tool** is a comprehensive overlay utility for MU Online featuring an in-game HUD, stat tracker, inventory helper, drop logger, party monitor, and hotkey menu.

Based on popular community tools and overlay frameworks for MU Online.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🛡️ Core Overlay
- Overlay HUD — display character stats without leaving the game
- Stat Tracker — live tracking of level, HP, mana, and experience
- Inventory Helper — quick item lookup and stash overview
- Drop Logger — record item drops with timestamps
- Party Monitor — track party members and their status

### 🎨 Interface & Hotkeys
- Hotkey Menu — configurable keybinds for every panel
- Custom Themes — adjust overlay colors and opacity
- Minimap Helper — compact map overlay for town and field
- Chat Filter — highlight important messages

### 🏋️ Utility & Training
- Session Timer — track playtime and farming sessions
- Experience Calculator — estimate time to next level
- Respawn Notifier — alert when boss or mob respawns
- Multi-Client Notes — keep notes for each game window
